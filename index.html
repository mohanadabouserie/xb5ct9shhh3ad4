Why Telegram, not WhatsApp

On Telegram the bot can message a coach or parent at any time, for free. WhatsApp charges a provider fee per number (360dialog's €49 a month) and, from 1 Oct 2026, Meta's Egyptian rate of $0.0036 per message past 1,000 free a month. The Sunday reports and attendance prompts are sent unprompted, so on WhatsApp they would also need pre-approved templates.

Measured here

Show the measurements
  • Typed note: 1,960 in / 173 out, from 8 notes in English, Arabic and Franco on 20 Oct (1,840–2,050).
  • Voice note: writing it down took ~270 tokens of instructions plus the audio (8.8s and 18.2s notes), ~4 tokens out per second; reading it then took 2,000 in / 517 out — spoken notes run longer and cover more.
  • Coach question: 2,110 + 5,300 in over two calls, 133 out (4 questions).
  • Parent answer: 3,250 in / 131 out (8 questions); relay to coach 162 / 159 on Flash Lite.
  • Report: 515 / 278 (10 kids); translation 340 / 484 (12, Arabic and Franco).

What costs nothing

Show the free parts
  • Every Telegram message, in or out, voice and photos included.
  • Attendance — the checklist 10 minutes before training is all buttons.
  • The Friday check asks with buttons; only the coach's spoken or typed answers count, already inside “notes per kid”.
  • Sending reports, kid photos, menus and language changes.
  • A translated report is saved, so a second parent in the same language costs nothing.

Deliberately excluded

No salaries, contractors or engineering time — infrastructure and usage only. Also outside: the computer's electricity if self-hosted, paid monitoring, an SMS or WhatsApp fallback for parents without Telegram, and any one-off data entry of kids and photos into the dashboard.
